Hey, I’m Utkarsh.

I grew up in a small town, watching how people move through markets and shops without really noticing them. A bachelor’s in Product design taught me how things are built. A year in industry taught me how they’re used. Now, in my master’s, I design stores, fixtures and brands for one simple goal: to make people stop and notice.
